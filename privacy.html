Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Entity: PropertiesEgy Real Estate Brokerage SAE • Jurisdiction: Arab Republic of Egypt
Google Ads & User Privacy Guarantee: PropertiesEgy adheres strictly to Google Ads Transparency Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Egyptian Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020. We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Welcome to PropertiesEgy ("we", "us", "our", "PropertiesEgy.com"). We operate an independent real estate consultancy and licensed brokerage in Egypt, providing personalized advisory and official developer direct sales support. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent practices regarding data collection, usage, and safeguarding when you use our website or engage our consulting services.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when requesting real estate consultations, unit brochures, price sheets, or telephone inquiries:

  • Identity Data: Full name, salutation, preferred language.
  • Contact Data: Mobile phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, country of residence.
  • Property Preferences: Desired destination (New Cairo, Sheikh Zayed, North Coast, Mostakbal City, New Administrative Capital), preferred developer (e.g. Hassan Allam, Palm Hills, Emaar, SODIC, etc.), property type, and approximate investment budget.
  • Technical & Browsing Data: IP address, device type, browser information, pages visited, and interaction data via Google Analytics and Google Ads conversion tags.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is processed strictly for legitimate business purposes:

  • To provide customized property portfolios, payment plans, and developer comparative analysis.
  • To schedule private compound viewings, developer showroom visits, and digital video tours for overseas buyers.
  • To communicate via phone, email, or WhatsApp regarding projects that match your explicit request.
  • To measure the effectiveness of Google Ads campaigns, site speed, and user experience.

3. 0% Buyer Commission & Independent Brokerage Status

PropertiesEgy provides buyers with advisory services at 0% commission. We are compensated directly by authorized developers based on standard industry contracts. Your data is only shared with a specific developer when you explicitly instruct us to coordinate an official unit reservation or contract signing in your name.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use essential and performance cookies (including Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager) to understand visitor journeys and optimize page speed. You have full control over cookie preferences via our on-screen consent banner or your browser settings.

5. Data Retention & Security

All transmitted information is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3/SSL). We implement robust technical, physical, and administrative safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of your contact records.

6. Your Rights

Under applicable international data privacy laws, you possess the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of any incomplete or inaccurate details.
  • Request deletion or erasure of your data from our active CRM systems.
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time by messaging "STOP" on WhatsApp or sending an email.
